## 1. Executive Overview

This digital campus platform is the official software system for **Shri Chhatrapati Shivaji College, Omerga**, representing the comprehensive data from the **Information Brochure 2026-27 (माहिती पुस्तिका २०२६-२७)**. Built on a modern full-stack architecture, it includes:
- **Public College Portal** featuring the authentic emblem of Bharat Shikshan Sanstha, President & Principal's message, 15 sister institutions, departments, labs, real fee structures, and online admission applications.
- **8 Dedicated Role Portals** (Student, Parent, Faculty, HOD, Principal, Sanstha Admin, Registrar/Exam Officer, Alumni).
- **Flagship Innovation Units**: ICICI Foundation RAC Laboratory, Geography Department Soil Testing Laboratory (माती परीक्षण केंद्र Room 13), Career Katta (C34650), NISM Financial Literacy, SCSC-ICE Incubation Center, Police & Military Academy, and 24x7 Night Study Room (रात्र अभ्यासिका).

## 5. Technology Architecture

- **Framework**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- **Visual Design**: Poppins font, authentic emblem of Bharat Shikshan Sanstha, Omerga
- **Charts**: Recharts visualizations for student strength, attendance, placement trends, and fee accounts
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM (`prisma/schema.prisma`)
- **Archived Package**: `SCSCO-College-Digital-Platform.zip`
